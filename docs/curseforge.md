Kept here so the project page and the README cannot drift apart.

## Summary

Short field, shown in search results and on the project card.

> Puts tank, healer and damage icons back on your party portraits. Blizzard only
> shows them in dungeon finder groups, so this one also works in a party you
> built by hand: it reads each member's specialization, and switches to the
> assigned role the moment the group has one. MoP Classic and Classic Era,
> Hardcore included.
